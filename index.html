The photo-finish party game

FaceRace is a free racing game you play with your friends' faces. Add 2–20 photos, pick a track theme and race length, and watch everyone sprint to a photo finish — with boosts, banana peels and a champions' leaderboard. There's also a wheel-of-names picker and a random number generator for quick decisions. No account, no download: it runs right in your browser and even works offline. Everything stays on your device — photos are never uploaded anywhere.

🏁 How to play

1

Add your racers

On the Setup tab, tap + Add racer to add 2–20 friends. Give each one a name and a photo — square photos look best as the circular avatars. Tap an avatar any time to edit it.

2

Save your crew (optional)

Tap 💾 Save to store the current line-up as a reusable group, then load it again later in one tap. Use 🔀 Shuffle to mix up the order.

3

Set the rules

Pick a race duration and a theme (track, beach, space and more). Toggle random events for surprise boosts & banana peels, flip on sound, or upload your own background.

4

Start the race

Hit the 🚦 Race! tab. After the countdown, watch everyone sprint to the photo finish. A podium and full results appear at the end — then tap 🔁 Rematch or share the result.

5

Spin or track stats

Need a quick winner instead of a full race? Use the 🎡 Picker wheel. Check the 📊 Stats leaderboard and 📜 History tabs to see who keeps winning.

🎲 It's all fair. Every racer has an equal chance to win — the finish order is random, so the photos, boosts and banana peels are just for fun.
